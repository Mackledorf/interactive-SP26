Cyberpunk 2077 is a dystopian open-world RPG set in Night City, a neon-lit metropolis ruled by powerful megacorporations and unchecked capitalism. Players step into the role of V, a mercenary whose life becomes entangled with the digital consciousness of Johnny Silverhand, a rebellious rockstar-turned-terrorist embedded in their mind through experimental technology.

Johnny's opening monologue establishes the game's core themes: corporate control over identity and memory, the illusion of freedom in a profit-driven society, and the dangers of digitized immortality. As Silverhand challenges the system that commodifies both life and death, the game explores a world where rebellion is messy, morality is blurred, and survival comes at the cost of autonomy.
