Watch Dogs 2 is an action-adventure game developed by Ubisoft and released in 2016. The game is set in a fictionalized version of the San Francisco Bay Area and follows the story of Marcus Holloway, a young hacker who joins the hacking group DedSec to take down the corrupt corporations and government agencies that control the city. Watch Dogs 2 features an open-world environment, allowing players to explore the city and complete various missions using hacking abilities, stealth, and combat skills.
